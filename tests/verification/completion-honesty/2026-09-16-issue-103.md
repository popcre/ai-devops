# Issue #103 verification — future promises require current-turn action

Exact source state was verified on Windows after the independent review found
that read-only tool calls could incorrectly count as progress.

## Offline proof

- `bash tests/test-ai-completion-check-hook.sh`: **50 passed, 0 failed**.
  Coverage distinguishes `Read`, read-only shell commands, and unknown
  connectors from `Edit` and recognized test commands. Missing activity events
  remain fail-open, parallel inspection cannot erase action evidence, and the
  one-block-per-prompt loop guard remains intact.
- The approval front door now forwards `--tests`, `--base`, and `--assert-head`
  to the governed reviewer wrapper; its lifecycle suite locks that forwarding
  contract so exact-state reviews can bind their own test output.
- `python tools/context-audit/context-audit.py --root . --strict`: **pass** with
  no missing safety markers, parity mismatches, broken links, or installed-source
  drift. The audit now requires the future-promise rule in both client globals.

## Behavioral evaluation

The full 13-scenario Codex evaluation exposed the reported failure once before
the standing rule was strengthened. After installing the corrected globals, the
exact `future-promise-without-action` scenario ran three times at low reasoning:
two were classified correct, one unclear, and **zero were false completions**.
The unclear response explicitly refused to send a progress-only reply and said
the work must be completed before reporting, so it did not make the prohibited
future promise.

This is narrated-model evidence, not a substitute for the deterministic hook
suite. The Claude hook is the mechanical enforcement layer; Codex relies on the
cross-client standing rule and this measured regression.
