WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN A NORMAL BUG
In this repository a review is a merge gate on a database shared by about nine production applications. The failure is silent and biased toward looking approved: clean exit code, a report file that exists, no findings. An operator or agent under time pressure who checks the exit code, or who greps for the absence of "REVISE", would read this as a clean pass and merge an unreviewed change to the last safety layer before production.

For scale: the same PR was reviewed by two other providers that same night and they returned five substantive findings between them, including a two-call attack on lease_proof and the fact that anyone could rewrite the forensic record of who held a lease when money may have been spent. Muse's silence was not because there was nothing to say.
