# Customer journeys

Select the journeys that match the requested role and scope. Prefer one complete
business flow over many shallow clicks.

## RFQ and factory quoting

1. Create or copy an RFQ using valid product classification, customer, delivery,
   quantity, case pack, description, and licensing information.
2. Attach factories or a factory group and prevent duplicate assignments.
3. Send one or many price requests and verify notifications or invitation links.
4. As the vendor, submit price, carton measurements, case pack, and supporting data.
5. As sourcing, compare responses, request a new price, keep a valid price, and
   choose the intended factory.
6. Verify status rules, calculated values, reload persistence, history, archive,
   restore, copy, export, saved views, and filtered bulk selection.

## Standardized products

- Create a product type, size, and version.
- Add or edit vendor data and pictures.
- Export selected standardized products into an RFQ.
- Verify highlighting, filters, named views, and spreadsheet export.

## Item Library and Item Detail

- Find, filter, open, create, edit, archive, and restore an item where permitted.
- Verify required fields and product classification selections.
- Add and remove attachments through supported controls.
- Review assignments, licensing status, comments, audit history, and reload persistence.

## Art Piece

- Create and classify an Art Piece, add artwork or attachments, edit details,
  archive or restore where supported, and open its linked product context.

## Licensing

- Filter licensed and generic products.
- Open the linked item, review status and feedback history, add permitted feedback
  or images, perform a safe bulk update, and verify persistence.
- Confirm vendors see only the licensing summary intended for their production order.

## Production

- Find a production order, inspect related item details, edit permitted milestone
  dates, save, reload, filter, and verify calculated or derived dates.
- As a vendor, select only an assigned factory and order, update permitted dates,
  and confirm another factory's order cannot be reached.

## Samples

- Exercise all available origins and paths: requested from factory, offered by
  factory, pulled from inventory, or returned by a customer.
- Create a sample, add photos and comments, use the phone upload link, and inspect history.
- Declare or receive quantity, pack into boxes, ship, deliver, return, repack,
  retain automatically, dispose, record loss, and correct discrepancies as permitted.
- Test NYC, Ningbo, factory, customer, and in-transit locations.
- Test local closeout and authorized reopen separately from global completion.
- Verify vendors are restricted to their own factory and office actions stay internal.

## Lead time

- Calculate a schedule using design count and product classification.
- Verify the design, licensing, sampling, and production breakdown.
- Edit permitted design-team, licensing, or factory timing reference rows and
  confirm recalculation and reload persistence.

## Administration

- Invite internal users and factory representatives, resend invitations, edit
  roles, configure access, impersonate each role, and return safely.
- Maintain customers and permitted master lists.
- Inspect audit logs, tariff tables, factory countries, Section 301 data, and AI
  settings without changing live external services during ordinary QA.
