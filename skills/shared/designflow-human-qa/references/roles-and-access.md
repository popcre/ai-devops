# Roles and access

Use this as the expected business-role map. Confirm actual access in the running
site because navigation visibility and server permissions have historically drifted.

## Admin

- Access every operational module.
- Manage users, invitations, factory representatives, role access, and saved layouts.
- Impersonate another user and clearly return to the admin session.
- Maintain customers, reference lists, factory countries, tariff data, and AI settings.
- Review audit history and controlled archive tools.

## Sourcing manager

- Create and classify RFQs and standardized products.
- Assign factories, send quote requests, compare responses, request new prices,
  and choose the winning factory.
- Enter factory cost, freight, duty, royalty rates, and target margins.
- Check that landed costs, selling prices, and resulting margins calculate correctly.
- Manage vendor groups, Item Library information, production, licensing, samples,
  lead times, and permitted operational settings.

## Sales

- Create and review RFQs and standardized products.
- Work with customers, product descriptions, selling-price fields, and Item Library records.
- Review commercial calculations, comments, mentions, notifications, exports, and saved views.
- Do not assume Sales has internal tracking access merely because a read service accepts the role.

## Designer

- Maintain product descriptions, artwork, Art Pieces, attachments, technical links,
  standardized products, and licensing information.
- Review RFQ details and production, licensing, and sample progress.
- Confirm read-only or restricted sample actions are not presented as writable.

## Production

- Maintain production-order dates, milestones, licensing progress, samples,
  boxes, shipments, closeouts, and lead-time data.
- Work with Item Library and Art Piece information needed for production.
- Maintain only the administrative reference information permitted to this role.

## Vendor or factory

- View only the factory's own RFQs, quotes, production orders, licensing summaries,
  samples, and factory representatives.
- Submit and update the factory's own quote price and carton information.
- Keep a price, respond to a new-price request, or bulk-upload factory offers.
- Update assigned production dates.
- Offer, declare, pack, ship, or repack the factory's own samples where supported.
- Never change internal RFQ status or another factory's records.

## Cross-role checks

- Login, logout, password recovery, Microsoft sign-in, expired sessions, and invitations.
- Navigation, direct-link access, role changes, impersonation banner, and return-to-admin.
- Profile, language preference, saved views, comments, mentions, notifications, and attachments.
- Direct links and mobile sample links reveal only the record and actions they authorize.
